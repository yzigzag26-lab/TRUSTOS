HOW TRUSTOS WORKS

From claim to evidence.

TRUSTOS turns a digital claim into an independently verifiable result using evidence from the underlying system of record.

01

Submit

Provide the blockchain transaction hash associated with the claim.

02

Verify

TRUSTOS independently queries the BNB Smart Chain and inspects the actual transaction data.

03

Prove

The result is presented as evidence that can be independently checked against the blockchain.

TRUSTOS PRINCIPLE

Never trust a claim when we can verify evidence.

Digital systems increasingly depend on claims made by people, organizations and machines. TRUSTOS is built around a simple principle: when verifiable evidence exists, trust should be backed by that evidence.
